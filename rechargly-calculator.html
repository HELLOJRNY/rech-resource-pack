These templates are designed to be sent ahead of your engagement renewal. The key principle: lead with the work you have been doing for the client, not the number going up. Be specific, be direct, and send them early. Clients who hear about a fee change well before their engagement letter arrives push back far less than those who find out in the letter itself.

Annual fee increase
Subject Your updated fees from [DATE]
Use when reviewing fees as part of your annual engagement rollover
Hi [CLIENT NAME], I wanted to touch base ahead of your engagement renewal to walk you through our updated fees for the coming year. Over the past 12 months we have [brief description of the work: e.g. managed your BAS and IAS across all four quarters, handled the end of year financials and tax return, and added monthly reporting to your package]. Our costs have also increased over that period and this adjustment reflects both. From [DATE] your monthly fee will move from $[CURRENT] to $[NEW]. Your updated engagement letter will follow shortly. Please give me a call if you have any questions before then. [NAME] [FIRM] [PHONE]
New service line
Subject [SERVICE NAME] now included in your engagement
Use when formalising a fee for a service you have been delivering without one, or introducing a new service such as AI-assisted reporting, cash flow forecasting, or CFO advisory work
Hi [CLIENT NAME], Over the past [period] we have been [brief description: e.g. using AI-assisted tools to improve the turnaround and accuracy of your quarterly reporting / providing monthly cash flow forecasts alongside your standard compliance work]. We are formalising this as a distinct part of your engagement. From [DATE] it will be included as a separate line item at $[FEE] per month. Your updated engagement letter will cover this. I am happy to jump on a call if you would like to talk through what is included. [NAME] [FIRM] [PHONE]
Software subscription pass-through
Subject A change to how your software costs are handled from [DATE]
Use when separating software subscription costs (such as Xero or MYOB) that have been bundled into your engagement fee and passing them on as a distinct line item
Hi [CLIENT NAME], From [DATE] we will be separating the cost of your [SOFTWARE] subscription from your monthly engagement fee and invoicing it as a separate line item at $[AMOUNT] per month. [If total cost stays the same:] Your engagement fee will reduce by the same amount so your total monthly cost stays the same. The change simply means your software cost and our service fee are clearly separated on your invoice. [If there is a net increase:] Your total monthly cost will increase by $[X] to reflect this. Until now this cost has been included in your engagement fee. We are separating it so both amounts are clearly visible. Your updated engagement letter will include this change. Please get in touch if you have any questions. [NAME] [FIRM] [PHONE]
